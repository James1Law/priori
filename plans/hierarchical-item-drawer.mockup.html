Item drawer hierarchy features:
  • Breadcrumb trail: Clickable links to navigate up the hierarchy
  • Level badge: Clear visual indicator of initiative/epic/story level
  • Parent selector: Dropdown to reparent the item (move to a different parent)
  • Children list: Shows direct children with status and estimate, clickable to navigate
  • Aggregate metrics: Parent items show rolled-up totals from children
  • Add sub-item: Button to create a child directly from the drawer
  • Max depth indicator: Level 2 (story) items show a message that no further nesting is possible
