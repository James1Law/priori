Backlog View

Prioritised List with Scope Line

A dedicated view for managing and communicating your prioritised backlog. Items are displayed as a ranked list with a draggable "cutoff line" to visually separate what's in scope from what's not. Supports both score-based auto-sorting and manual drag-and-drop reordering.

UX Notes - Desktop

  • View Tabs: Scoring (frameworks) | Backlog (this view) | Roadmap (timeline, future)
  • Drag handles: Items can be reordered by dragging the ⋮⋮ handle
  • Cutoff Line: Draggable between any two items. Label is editable (click to rename)
  • Below-line styling: Greyed out to show "out of scope" status
  • Score badge: Shows score from whichever framework is active in Scoring view
  • Order modes:
    • Score (default): Auto-sorted by framework score, highest first
    • Manual: User has dragged items to custom positions (amber indicator)
  • Reset to Score: Button appears when in Manual mode. Clicking resets to score order.
