Roadmap hierarchy design:
  • Top 3 levels shown: Goal → Initiative → Epic on the timeline. Stories/subtasks are too granular for roadmap view.
  • Parent bars are taller, bolder, and auto-span from earliest to latest child
  • Child bars are thinner with lighter colour variant
  • Collapsed parents show just the parent bar spanning the full range
  • Unscheduled panel groups items by parent hierarchy
  • Dragging a parent auto-moves all children proportionally
  • Matches production layout: bottom tab bar, "Add Item" top-right, "Add Period" link, sort hint at bottom
