Design decisions

Left colour accent bar replaces the level badge taking horizontal space. Pink=Goal, Blue=Initiative, Purple=Epic, Amber=Story, Slate=Subtask. No bar for flat items.

Title on second line — uses line-clamp-2 so titles can wrap to 2 lines before truncating. Always readable.

Reduced indent — 0.75rem per level, capped at 2.25rem max. Enough to show nesting without eating all the space.

Rank badge hidden on mobile for hierarchy items — the level badge + indent already communicates position.

Drag handle hidden on mobile — long-press to reorder (existing behaviour).

Badges shrunk — 10px text, 1.5px padding. Smaller but still legible.
